Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

What cookies are

Cookies (and similar browser storage like localStorage) are small pieces of data a website stores on your device so it can remember things between visits or page loads — like keeping you signed in, or remembering what's in your cart.

What we use, and why

Strictly necessary (always on): your login session (via Supabase Authentication) so you stay signed in as you browse, and your shopping cart contents, so items you add aren't lost between pages. These can't be switched off without breaking core site functionality like checkout.

Preference: a marker remembering that you've already seen and dismissed our homepage welcome popup, so it doesn't show again every visit.

Analytics: Google Analytics (GA4), which sets its own cookies to help us understand which pages are visited and how the site is used in aggregate. We don't use this to identify you personally.

Third-party cookies

Google Analytics may set its own cookies as described above. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google's own sign-in flow may briefly set cookies during that process, governed by Google's own policies. We don't run third-party advertising cookies or ad-retargeting pixels on this site.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser's settings at any time. Doing so may prevent you from staying logged in or keeping items in your cart between visits, since those rely on the "strictly necessary" storage described above. You can also opt out of Google Analytics specifically using Google's own browser add-on.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if what the site uses changes; the "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
