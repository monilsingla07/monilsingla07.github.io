Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information AhamStree ("we", "us") collects when you use www.ahamstree.com, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Account & contact details: name, email address, and phone number, when you create an account, sign in (including with Google), save a profile, or check out as a guest.
Shipping details: delivery address and pincode, provided at checkout, used to ship your order and to check delivery estimates.
Order information: the products you buy, order value, and order status/history.
Payment information: we do not collect or store your card, UPI, or netbanking details ourselves — payments are processed directly by Razorpay, our payment processor, under its own privacy and security practices.
Return evidence: if you request a return for a manufacturing defect, the photos you upload as evidence.
Usage data: pages viewed, general device/browser information, and site interactions, collected automatically via Google Analytics (see Cookies below), and separately through our own internal traffic log — the pages you visit, the site that referred you, device type, your IP address, and an approximate city/country derived from that IP. This internal log exists so we can see visitor activity in our own admin tools rather than relying solely on Google Analytics.
Login history: each time you sign in, we record the date/time, the approximate method used (Google, WhatsApp OTP, or email), and the IP address and device/browser the sign-in came from. This is kept for account security — for example, to help us investigate unauthorised access to an account.

3. How we use your information

To process and ship your orders and invoices; to communicate with you about an order, a return, or a support request; to prevent fraud and abuse (for example, limiting repeated misuse of a discount code, or investigating suspicious sign-in activity on an account); to improve the site based on aggregate usage patterns and our own internal traffic log; and to comply with tax and legal record-keeping requirements (for example, GST invoicing).

4. Who we share it with

We share only what's needed to fulfil your order, with: Razorpay (to process payment), Shiprocket and its courier partners (to ship your order and provide tracking — this includes your name, phone, and delivery address), Supabase (our database and authentication provider, which stores your account, order, traffic-log, and login-history data on our behalf), Google (for Google Analytics, and for Google Sign-In if you choose to use it), and a third-party IP-geolocation lookup service used only to derive an approximate city/country from an IP address for our internal traffic log and login history (no other data is shared with it). We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or other third parties.

5. Cookies & similar technology

See our Cookie Policy for the specific cookies/local storage used for your cart, login session, and analytics.

6. Data retention

We keep order and invoice records for as long as required under Indian tax and accounting law. Account information is kept while your account is active; you can ask us to delete it as described below, subject to what we're legally required to retain (for example, completed order/invoice records). Internal traffic-log and login-history entries are kept for a limited period for security and site-improvement purposes and are not sold or used for advertising.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask what we hold, by reaching out through our Contact Us page. You can also update your name, phone, and address yourself at any time from your account's Profile page.

8. Data security

We rely on our providers' security controls (Supabase for data storage and authentication, Razorpay for payment processing) rather than handling sensitive data like passwords or card numbers ourselves in plain text. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, can be sent via our Contact Us page.
